After Hours Drink

For the Drink:

1.5 oz (45 ml) Bombay Dry Gin
0.75 oz (22.5 ml) Fresh Lemon Juice
0.75 oz (22.5 ml) Earl Grey–Infused Pear Syrup (recipe below)
0.5 oz (15 ml) Licor 43
2 dashes Angostura Bitters

Garnish: Dehydrated pear wheel, fresh lemon wheel, or a star anise pod.

1. How to Make the Earl Grey–Infused Pear Syrup
Ingredients:

1 cup Pear Juice or Pear Nectar (Bosc or Bartlett pears work best if juicing fresh)
1 cup Sugar
2 Earl Grey tea bags (or 2 tbsp loose-leaf Earl Grey)

Instructions:

Heat the Base: In a small saucepan, bring the pear juice and sugar to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring until the sugar completely dissolves.

Infuse Tea: Remove the pan from the heat. Add the Earl Grey tea bags and let steep for 5 to 7 minutes (do not over-steep, or the tea will become overly astringent/tannic).

Cool & Store: Remove the tea bags without squeezing them excessively. Let the syrup cool completely to room temperature, then pour it into a clean jar or glass bottle. Keep refrigerated for up to 3–4 weeks.

2. Assembling & Mixing the Cocktail

Chill your glass: Place a coupe or Nick & Nora glass into the freezer for a few minutes while you assemble the drink.

Combine ingredients: In a cocktail shaker, add the Bombay Dry Gin, Licor 43, Earl Grey–infused pear syrup, fresh lemon juice, and Angostura bitters.

Shake: Fill the shaker with plenty of ice. Seal tightly and shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds until properly chilled and diluted.

Strain: Double-strain (using a Hawthorne strainer and a fine-mesh tea strainer) into your chilled glass to filter out ice shards.

Garnish: Float a thin slice of pear or lemon wheel on top.
