Duross Potato Salad

Bill tried to determine amounts as he just wings it but here’s the general recipe.  He has no idea how much mayonnaise he uses - just mixes and adds more if needed.

5 lbs potatoes (Idaho or russet)
1/4 - 1/2 cup sweet relish (to taste)
6-8 hard boiled eggs
2-4 scallions (to taste)
Salt and pepper (to taste)
Mayonnaise 

Wash the potatoes (keep skin on).
Generously poke the potatoes.
Boil the potatoes in salted water until soft/done.
Cool completely and then peel.
Cut into 1/2” cubes.
Then mix all the ingredients together in a large bowl.  Refrigerate.
