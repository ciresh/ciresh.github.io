ORGEAT SYRUP
3 cups white sugar
2 cups unsweetened almond milk**
1 tsp orange blossom water
1/2 tsp rose water
1/2 tsp almond extract 
In a medium sauce pan, combine sugar and almond milk. Stir over heat until sugar is completely dissolved. Remove from heat and add orange blossom water, rose water, and almond extract. Stir to combine.


Quick and Easy Orgeat
2 cups plain unsweetened almond milk
4 cups granulated sugar
2 Tbsp almond extract
1 tsp orange flower water
1/3 cup cognac

In a medium saucepan, heat the almond milk and sugar over medium-high heat, stirring until the sugar is dissolved, about 3-5 minutes. Remove from heat, stir in the almond extract, orange flower water, and cognac. Let cool. Transfer to an airtight bottle or jar and keep refrigerated.

I will second all of this...Smith and Cross is a great rum for a Mai Tai, IMO. Also, homemade orgeat can be very easy...and it allows you to tweak the flavors if you want to. Here's my method:
1 cup of Almond milk
1-1/2 cups of white sugar
1/4 cup (2 oz) cognac
1 tsp almond extract
1/4 to 1/2 tsp orange flower water (I do just a few drops)

Add all ingredients (except orange flower water) into a saucepan and heat on med-high heat, stirring until sugar is dissolved. Once sugar is completely dissolved, remove from heat (for about 5-10 min), then add the orange flower water and transfer to a sealable container (like a mason jar)
