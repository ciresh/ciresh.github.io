Flannel Shirt

1 3/4 oz Scotch whisky (blended or Speyside recommended)
1 1/2 oz fresh cloudy apple cider
1/2 oz Amaro Averna
1/4 oz fresh lemon juice
1 tsp Demerara syrup (2:1 ratio)
1/2 tsp St. Elizabeth Allspice Dram
2 dashes Angostura aromatic bitters

Demerara Syrup

Mix 2 parts dark brown sugar with 1 part water.
Example: Gently heat 1/2 cup dark brown sugar and 1/4 cup water
